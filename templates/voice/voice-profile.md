## 0. The findings that matter most

Five to nine habits that make this writer recognizable, most important
first. For each: what they do, two quoted examples with doc IDs, and how
often. Things to look for:
- how pieces are scaffolded (question ladders, "Firstly / Lastly", headers);
- how they open (scene, number, quote, claim);
- how they concede and turn ("It is true that... However,");
- how they ground numbers or abstractions;
- connective texture ("due to the fact that", "Thus,", "This shows");
- punctuation signatures (dash asides, semicolons, colons before questions);
- how they conclude (restated recap, question, metaphor);
- recurring slips (record them; never reproduce them).
