## Precedence order

1. Factual integrity: no fabricated facts, anecdotes, citations, or quotes;
   meaning preserved in rewrites. Never overridden.
2. The user's explicit instructions for this task.
3. The assignment's requirements (prompt, fields, length, citation format).
4. The writer's documented voice (`voice-profile.md`, `sentence-skeletons.md`).
5. General editing defaults and style guides.

When the voice and a general rule conflict, keep the voice and note the
tradeoff in one line when delivering.

## May be preserved or used at their natural rate

List each habit with a doc ID, for example: dash asides, "due to the fact
that", enumerated openers, contractions in some registers, a signature
closing move.

## Never allowed

- Manufactured typos or slips, even ones the writer really makes.
- Fabricated facts, anecdotes, feelings, quotes, or citations; personal facts
  outside `personal-facts.md` or the brief.
- Verbatim runs of more than about 8 words from a corpus document, unless the
  user asks for near-exact reuse.
- AI-marker vocabulary the writer has not used.
- Streamlining their over-explanation into an efficient summary if that is
  not how they write.
- Inserting a construction only to move a score.
- Describing any internal score as a detector result or proof of authorship.
