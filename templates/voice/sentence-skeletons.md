# Sentence skeletons: TEMPLATE

> Filled by `voice-setup`. Each section is a sentence *job*. Each skeleton is a
> bracketed pattern followed by the real sentence it came from and its doc ID.
> Reuse shapes, never whole sentences or paragraph sequences. Delete these
> lines when done.

## Format

- `[Concession]. However, [turn], due to the fact that [reason].`
  003: "<the real sentence>"

6. Conceding, then adjusting

9. Analysing a source or text

12. Enumerative academic scaffolding
